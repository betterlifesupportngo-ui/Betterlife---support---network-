Applicant Requirements

Requirements can vary by programme and should be confirmed before submission.

Applicant information

  • Valid identity and contact information
  • Residential information
  • Employment or business details
  • Purpose and amount requested
  • Accurate financial information

Guarantor

  • A qualified guarantor may be required
  • UK citizen or valid UK working, residential or settled status
  • Contact and referee information
  • Consent to act as guarantor

Supporting documents

  • Identity evidence where required
  • Address evidence where required
  • Relevant business or employment evidence
  • Supporting financial information
Security reminder: Never upload passports, identity documents, bank statements or other sensitive information to a public GitHub repository or public spreadsheet.
