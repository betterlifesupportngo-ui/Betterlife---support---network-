Applicant Requirements

Requirements may include the following, subject to final programme rules and applicable law.

Applicant

  • Valid identity information
  • Contact and residential details
  • Employment or business information
  • Purpose and amount requested
  • Accurate financial information

Guarantor

  • Qualified guarantor where required
  • UK citizen or valid UK working/residential/settled status
  • Contact and referee information
  • Consent to act as guarantor

Documents

  • Identity evidence where required
  • Address evidence where required
  • Relevant business/employment evidence
  • Supporting financial information
Never upload identity, bank, passport or other highly sensitive documents to a public GitHub repository or public spreadsheet.
