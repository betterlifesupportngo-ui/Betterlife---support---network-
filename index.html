OUR PURPOSE

Practical support for people building something better.

We aim to provide a clear, structured pathway for applicants seeking financial support for livelihoods, employment and business activity.

01

Accessible

A straightforward application pathway with clear requirements and programme information.

02

Responsible

Requests are subject to eligibility, assessment, available funding and formal written terms.

03

Transparent

Applicants should be able to understand the process, obligations and limitations before proceeding.

HOW IT WORKS

A clear process from application to agreement.

Applications are reviewed before any support is approved. If an application is approved, the applicable conditions are documented in a written agreement.
