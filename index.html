OUR MISSION

Better support for better futures.

We aim to make access to structured financial support clearer and more accessible for people working to improve their livelihoods, businesses and communities.

Our approach is centred on transparency, responsible assessment and treating applicants with dignity throughout the process.

OUR PROGRAMME

Support built around real needs.

The programme is intended for eligible applicants whose proposed use of funds meets the programme's criteria.

01

Business support

For eligible business owners seeking funding to strengthen, expand or develop a viable enterprise.

02

Livelihood support

For qualifying individuals with a clear and responsible purpose for requested financial support.

03

Professional support

Applications from eligible civil servants and other working professionals may be considered under programme criteria.
