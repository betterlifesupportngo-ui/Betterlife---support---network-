Support Programme

Requests may be made for up to £500,000. Approval is not guaranteed and every request is subject to assessment.

Indicative term

Up to 18 months, subject to the signed agreement.

Repayments

Monthly repayments are contemplated, with repayment beginning after three months under the stated programme terms.

Initial repayment

The stated programme draft provides for a first repayment of 10% of the approved amount after three months.

Legal note: Any interest, late charge, penalty or guarantor consequence must be expressly stated in the signed agreement and comply with applicable UK law.
