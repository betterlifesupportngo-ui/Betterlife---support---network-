REPAYMENT FRAMEWORK

Understand the draft repayment structure.

The current draft framework describes monthly repayments beginning after three months.

MONTH 0

Agreement

Approved applicants receive written terms before any funding is released.

MONTH 3

First repayment

The draft terms provide for a first repayment of 10% of the approved amount after three months.

THEREAFTER

Monthly repayments

The remaining balance is repaid according to the schedule in the signed agreement.
