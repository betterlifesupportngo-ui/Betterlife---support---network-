How It Works

1. Check requirements

Review eligibility, guarantor and supporting-document requirements before applying.

2. Submit information

Provide accurate information and only use a secure application channel for sensitive documents.

3. Review

Your application may be assessed against programme requirements and available funding.

4. Decision

A decision is communicated with applicable conditions where relevant.

5. Agreement

Approved applicants receive written terms before any funds are released.

6. Repayment

Repayments follow the schedule and obligations in the signed agreement.
