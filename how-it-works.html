How It Works

A simple overview of the intended application journey.

STEP 01

Review requirements

Check eligibility, guarantor expectations and supporting information before applying.

STEP 02

Apply

Provide accurate personal, employment or business and support information.

STEP 03

Assessment

The request may be assessed against programme requirements and available funding.

STEP 04

Decision

Applicants are informed of the outcome and any applicable conditions.

STEP 05

Written agreement

Approved applicants review and accept the formal terms before funding.

STEP 06

Repayment

Repayments follow the schedule and obligations in the signed agreement.
