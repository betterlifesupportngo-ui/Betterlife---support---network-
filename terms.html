Applicant Terms & Conditions

1. Application

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or funding.

2. Guarantor

A qualified guarantor may be required. The guarantor should be a UK citizen or have valid UK working, residential or settled status and may act as a referee. Any legal liability must be set out in the signed agreement.

3. Term and repayment

The indicative term is 18 months. Repayments are monthly, beginning after three months. The draft programme provides for a first repayment of 10% of the approved amount after three months, with the remaining balance governed by the signed agreement.

4. Interest and penalties

The programme is described as 0% interest, subject to its terms. Any interest, late charge or penalty after the stated period must be expressly agreed in writing and permitted by applicable law.

5. Legal review

These draft terms are not a substitute for legal advice and should be reviewed by a qualified UK solicitor before real applications or lending activity begins.
