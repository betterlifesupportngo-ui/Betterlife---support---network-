About Betterlife Support Network

Betterlife Support Network is presented as a community-focused support initiative based in Scotland, focused on helping people and businesses pursue sustainable opportunities.

Our mission

To create practical pathways that can help people strengthen livelihoods, develop businesses and improve financial opportunity.

Our approach

Clear requirements, documented assessment and written terms are central to responsible support.

Transparency

We do not claim to be a bank, government body, registered charity or regulated lender unless the relevant legal status has been independently established.
